# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Mixed audience across Central America (Guatemala, El Salvador, Honduras, Belice, Costa Rica, Nicaragua):

- **Productores y gerentes de finca**: often on a phone, in the field, under direct sun and on slow connections. Many are older; they want to know quickly whether Agrologico has what they need and how to reach a person.
- **Compradores corporativos / agroindustria**: evaluating a regional supplier for larger projects; they look for track record (18+ años), represented brands, regional coverage and technical capability.

The job of the page: build trust and get the visitor to start a conversation (WhatsApp, phone or the quote form).

## Product Purpose

Single-page landing for Agrologico, a Central American agroindustrial supplier: irrigation systems and equipment, hoses, valves, protected crops, automation/ferti-riego, project design and related solutions. Success = qualified contacts and quote requests, mostly via WhatsApp.

## Brand Personality

Cercana y del campo, moderna e innovadora. Speaks the producer's language ("al alcance de todos") while signalling technology and precision (agricultura de precisión, IA, energía solar). Warm and human, never folksy; modern, never cold-tech. Tagline: "Agua. Campo. Futuro." Closing line: "Por un mejor planeta Tierra."

Visual reference: [caudal.es](https://caudal.es/) — clean full-bleed agricultural photography, confident sans-serif headlines, generous whitespace, product shots on soft backgrounds, B2B-professional but approachable.

## Anti-references

- **Agro cliché**: green-and-leaves decoration, rustic beige, farm illustrations, clip-art tractors.
- Generic SaaS/startup look (gradient text, metric cards, glassmorphism) — it disconnects from the field.
- Ferretería-style dense catalog grids with sale banners.

## Design Principles

1. **Polish, don't reinvent.** The current structure, palette (verde Agrologico + acento sol) and Montserrat typography are the brand; refine them.
2. **Legible under the sun.** High contrast, generous type sizes and tap targets; nothing important in light gray.
3. **Photography carries the story.** Real fields, real equipment; UI stays quiet around it.
4. **Every section leads to a conversation.** Contact (WhatsApp, phone, form) is always one step away.
5. **Light and fast on mobile.** Optimised images, minimal JS, motion that never blocks content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA: ≥4.5:1 body text, visible focus, full keyboard navigation, real labels on form fields.
- Mobile-in-the-field: strong contrast for outdoor glare, fast on slow 3G/4G.
- Older users: body text ≥16px, tap targets ≥44px, plain Spanish copy.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for every animation.
